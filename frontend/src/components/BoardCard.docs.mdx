## When to use

Use `BoardCard` wherever Boards are browsed as a grid of cards (#1978). Clicking anywhere on the
card calls `onOpen(board)`.

## Implementation guidance

- The cover is `BoardCover` in `banner` mode: full width at the 140px cover height of category
  and collection cards. It shows the pinned cover, else the first member Marker's thumbnail, else
  the filled Boards icon on brand red — the same no-cover treatment as a category's folder.
- The outlined Boards icon sits left of the brand-red title (clamped to two lines), like the
  category folder icon. The scope chip is the shared `CollectionVisibilityChip`.
- The whole face is a `CardActionArea`, so the card gets MUI's ripple on press and the
  hover/focus highlight category cards use. There is no border and no persistent selected state.
