<Title />

Real OpenSeadragon viewer with a DOM-portal marker layer, independent of Fabric.
Marker icons and title labels have fixed CSS-pixel dimensions. Only anchor
positions change on animation, open, resize, rotation and flip events; labels
and icons remain upright.

**Rotate and zoom** demonstrates constant sizing. **Toggle independent
visibility** leaves one label without its marker and another marker without its
label. Both default to visible. Clicking a marker restores its saved position,
zoom and rotation, highlights it, and opens its details. The toolbar's compass
button arms persistent quick-click placement while pan/zoom stay available.
Escape cancels the draft first, then disarms the tool. Markers are 27px white
circular discs with vibrant blue glyphs, a subtle ring and shadow; their existing title labels are unchanged. Marker clicks
open the information card, with an explicit pencil for editing.

The shared artwork is `public/inuksuk-marker.svg`. Both saved and draft markers
use it, staying upright and constant-size under zoom, rotation and flip.

## Permanent panel integration

Visibility and placement stories now use the right-side viewer panel. Canvas
presses and Escape no longer dismiss it. A new placement selects its Inuksuks
content, and floating placement/info cards leave the closed toggle unobstructed.
