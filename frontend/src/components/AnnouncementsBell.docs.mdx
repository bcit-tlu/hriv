## Behaviour

- `unseenCount` drives the badge (capped at 99+); `announcements` is the shared `useAnnouncements`
  feed, filtered here to unseen live posts and capped at five (`BELL_PEEK_LIMIT`).
- Clicking a title calls `onOpenAnnouncements(id)` (page focused on that card); "See all" calls it
  with no id. The empty state reads "You're all caught up."
