## When to use

`App` renders the info bar directly below the regular image viewer. The image title leads on the
left, followed by the note clamped to one line with a "…more" link, then — pushed right — a
"Collections:" cell that shows as many names as fit and collapses the rest behind a "+ N more"
link. Both affordances open a `ViewportDialog` centred over the viewer. The bar always renders
because the title is always shown; the viewer instructions live in the `ImageDetailsDrawer`
footer.
