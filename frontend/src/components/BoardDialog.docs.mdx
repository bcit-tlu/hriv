The in-place Board dialog used when a Marker drawer Board heading is clicked:
the Board opens over the image being viewed instead of navigating to Boards &
Markers. Marker tiles still jump to their saved views, favourite toggles work,
and editable Boards keep the Edit pencil plus owner management — all through the
same BoardEditDialog, BoardOwnersDialog and MarkerFormDialog used elsewhere.

The heavier management flows (reordering, move/save, remove-from-board, bulk
selection and Collection creation) stay on the Boards & Markers page, reachable
via the “Open in Boards & Markers” action.
