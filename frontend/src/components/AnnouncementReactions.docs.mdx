## Behaviour

- Pills show every reaction with a count; the viewer's own are filled (`aria-pressed`).
- The picker offers the eight allowlisted ids from `ANNOUNCEMENT_REACTIONS`; emoji are engagement
  only, never kinds.
- Without an `onToggleReaction` handler, or when the post is not live, pills are read-only and the
  picker is hidden; with no reactions at all the component renders nothing.
