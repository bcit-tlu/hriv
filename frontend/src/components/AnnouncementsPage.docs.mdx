## Behaviour

- **Inbox** lists everything the viewer may see (`GET /api/announcements/?scope=inbox`) via the
  shared `useAnnouncements` feed the bell also uses. Live, unseen posts are marked seen once they
  are on screen — once per `(id, notified_at)` — and stay in the list, rendered quieter.
- **Mine** (`canCompose` only) loads `?scope=mine`: Draft / Scheduled / Live / Expired chips filter
  the list; rows with `can_edit` get Edit / Duplicate / Delete, co-managed rows are read-only.
- Delete always confirms first. Duplicate opens the composer with "(copy)" and no schedule.
- `focusId` (from `?id=` or the bell peek) scrolls to and highlights one card.
- `composeGroupId` ("Post to group" in the Groups dialog) opens the composer with that group
  pre-selected once the instructor's programs and groups have loaded.

Staff stories are omitted here because Mine and the composer need `/api/programs` and
`/api/groups`; see `AnnouncementComposer` for the authoring states.
