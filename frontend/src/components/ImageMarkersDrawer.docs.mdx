The Room trigger is an auto-fading TOP_LEFT OpenSeadragon control.
It has no number badge, matching adjacent white-on-dark viewer controls.
The left-anchored panel stops above the toolbar and stays inside the viewer in
full-page mode and shares its existing image-data request with the markers.

The redundant Markers (total) heading is removed in both the legacy drawer and
the shared viewer panel. Rows group under their associated Boards: the markers
hook pages through the visible-Board summaries and each Marker lists under
every Board in its board_ids. Groups are tiered by ownership — the viewer's
own Boards first, then instructor or program Boards, while remaining visible
Boards sit inside a collapsed Public & Shared section (it expands on click or
when a shared row is focused) whose markers start hidden on the canvas.
Group headings are body2 titles, never
collapsible and carry no counts; a Board heading is a link
(`Open Board: {name}`) that opens `BoardDialog` in place over the viewer —
the same title, scope/owner chrome, marker grid and edit affordances as the
Boards & Markers dialog, with an “Open in Boards & Markers” escape to the
full management page. Markers with no visible Board fall under a trailing Other group,
and an open draft sits in a leading Unsaved group.

Each row's ⋮ menu starts
with Show/Hide marker, then Show/Hide label and permitted Edit/Delete.
Hide/Show label(s) use LabelOff/Label icons. Group headings carry inline eye and
label icon buttons instead of a ⋮ menu. A hidden marker always hides its label —
the row and group label controls disable while markers are hidden — while a shown
marker's label toggles independently. Group actions write the choice as a row
override on every member of that group in one batched update, leaving other
Boards and other images untouched; a later row action overrides the group.
Mixed groups show
accessible “Mixed” states (aria-pressed); clicking a mixed group shows all.
A row navigates without opening an editor, and selecting a canvas-hidden row
reveals the marker — selection always lands on a visible marker, matching the
`?marker=` navigation used by Boards & Markers and search. The explicit owner edit
action selects/focuses and pans to the Marker and opens its top-right in-place
editor, not a central dialog. Delete opens the shared confirmation dialog alone,
without the information card. Outside
presses preserve an open editor until Save/Cancel or explicit Escape. The
information card has no close X. Rows have titles only; drafts appear in a
leading Unsaved group. Escape restores focus to the trigger when no draft needs cancelling.
Hover backgrounds span the full row, including the separate options button.
Hidden-marker titles use text.disabled (contrast-safe text.secondary in light mode)
and an accessible “marker hidden” suffix; the row stays navigable.

Pin/Unpin mirrors My collections: pinned panels have no close button and stay open
across image and sequence navigation, and reopen on new viewer pages. Unpinning
keeps the panel open. The user-scoped localStorage preference is
`hrivpref:markers-drawer:pinned:user:<id>` (anonymous previews use `anonymous`).
Unpinned panels retract on outside presses/clicks, including the OSD canvas, without
consuming placement clicks. Their menu portals count as inside; the stable viewer
portal container survives menu exit transitions. Escape closes temporary panels only.

Preferences are scoped to the signed-in user, bounded to 500 recent overrides,
and shared across synchronized panes and browser tabs. Sequence navigation
arrows hide while this panel is open, rather than painting over its rows.

## Current image and Sequence panel

The pin/click-away drawer above is now the legacy Synchronized-pane interface.
Image and Sequence viewers with Markers enabled instead render the exported
`ImageMarkersList` inside `ViewerSidePanel`, without this drawer's header, pin,
close button or dismissal handlers. The groups, full-width hover, dimmed titles,
visibility preferences and stable menu portal remain shared. Persistent and
Permanent stories cover navigation persistence and non-dismissal on outside
presses/Escape. Synchronized continues to exercise the legacy interface.
All viewer variants keep My and Instructor markers prominent in both the list
and the canvas. Markers that only reach the viewer through a classmate's public
or shared Board stay in the collapsed Public & Shared section and render hidden
on the canvas until the viewer opts in; they are also browsable through
Boards & Markers → All Boards → Public & Shared, without changing backend
visibility.
