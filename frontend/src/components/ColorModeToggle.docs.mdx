## When to use

Use `ColorModeToggle` in the app bar and on the login screen to let users cycle through light, dark, and auto theme preferences.

## Interactive example

<Primary />

<Controls of={ColorModeToggleStories.Basic} />

## Guidance

- Light preference shows the light-mode icon and advances to dark on click.
- Dark preference shows the dark-mode icon and advances to auto on click.
- Auto preference shows the auto icon and follows the system-resolved colour mode.
- Use inherited/contrast colour in the app bar and `text.secondary` on the login screen.
