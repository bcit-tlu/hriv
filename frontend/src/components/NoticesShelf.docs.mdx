## Behaviour

- `selectNotices` keeps pinned + live posts from the shared feed, capped at three, in feed order.
- Card titles call `onOpenAnnouncements(id)`; "All announcements" opens the page unfocused.
- Bodies are never truncated: pinned posts must carry an expiry (#1953), so they cannot linger.
- Unseen posts show in full; seen posts collapse to their title row. A **Close** / **Expand** button sits at
  the top right, after the pin; closing an unseen post calls `onMarkSeen`, and a re-notify (new `notified_at`) re-expands it.
- The #1655 welcome message is a seeded pinned `everyone` announcement — no separate component.
