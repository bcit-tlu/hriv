# Inuksuks & Boards

An **Inuksuk** bookmarks a precise view of an image: its position, zoom and rotation.
A **Board** arranges these saved views into an ordered study.
These controls appear only when your deployment enables Inuksuks and Boards.

## Save and revisit a view

Open an image, move to the detail you want to keep, and use **Add Inuksuk**.
Give the Inuksuk a title and optional description. Reopening it restores that exact
view rather than the whole image. Saving an Inuksuk does not modify the source image.

## Organize a Board

Open **Boards**, create a Board, and add saved views. Reorder cards to arrange the study. Board owners can edit
their Boards; visibility and program/group restrictions determine who can view them.
Sharing a Board does not grant access to a restricted source image.

## Find saved views

Use the main **Search** dialog and enter words from a title or description.
The **Boards** and **Inuksuks** chips narrow the results. Choose a Board to open it,
or choose an Inuksuk to open its source image at the saved view. Search only returns
Boards and Inuksuks you can view.

## Turn saved views into a Collection

Use a Board's **Create Sequence Collection** action to make an ordered
walkthrough. In **Select** mode, select any number of cards for a Sequence, or 2–4
cards for a Synchronized comparison. You can also add selected views to an existing
Collection, subject to that Collection's limits and your editing permissions.

In a Collection's Manage dialog, search for a Board or Inuksuk and choose its result
to stage saved views into the draft. Review and save the draft to apply the additions.
Choosing a Board stages its visible views in Board order.

Each Collection slot keeps its own label, thumbnail and viewport, even when several
slots use the same source image. Changing a Collection's saved view does not change
the original Inuksuk. See [Collections](collections) for the viewer controls.
